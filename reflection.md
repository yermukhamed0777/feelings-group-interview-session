# Design reflection

The camera mount presented the hardest trade-off. The cradle has to retain a rounded triangular housing throughout a pan while keeping the optical face, vents and cable clear. A V-seat locates the housing and two straps provide retention. The pivot and linkage serve different roles, so each can be checked independently.

For a second iteration, the first step would be to trace the camera profile, measure its mass and locate its cable exit. A simple full-scale seat sample would allow a fit check before building the servo carrier. The next motion study would include the real servo horn, cable and straps at both travel limits.
