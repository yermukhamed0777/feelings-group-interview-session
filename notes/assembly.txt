Feelings lab / three mounting concepts / 18 November 2026
Units: mm. All housing and hardware sizes below are design assumptions pending measurement.

DISPLAY. The tray contact plane is 300 mm above the mounting plane. The assumed housing is 140 x 55; pocket allowance is 0.6 per side. Anchor the base at four holes. The 30 x 30 x 2 metal tube is 287 long. Retain the end sockets with M5 through-bolts and nuts. Check the low ports, screen border, vents, housing play and overturning loads before fabrication. The tray allows vertical removal.

PAW. For a nominal 27 x 21 body at its insertion section. The paw head remains exposed. Rest the device on its two outer edges. Pocket clearance is 0.6 per side. The front slot continues through the base so the USB cable can leave horizontally. Check the plug overmould, actual corner radii, button access and cable bend. Intended for a nearby table.

CAMERA. Concept for a nominal 112 wide x 44 deep x 98 tall rounded triangular camera, point down, lens forward. Place the two lower slopes on the V seat. Thread two separate soft straps through the two 11 x 3 slots and over solid housing edges; confirm lenses, vents, buttons and cables stay clear. Retention depends on these straps. Anchor base and spacers. Use an 8 mm smooth pivot shank, thrust washer, upper washer and locking nut with running clearance. Check pivot head recess against selected hardware. Use the rear diagonal drive hole with the pushrod. An externally supplied positional servo with an 18 mm crank drives the linkage. Verify servo mounting pattern, shaft offset, crank and pin heights, torque and travel. Use pin spacers as needed. Controller, servo, horn, straps and fasteners are purchased hardware. This concept provides limited-angle pan. Check linkage, cable sweep and strap retention through the selected range before powered operation. No rotation range has been validated.

FILES. Each FCStd opens independently. The STEP files are assembly exchanges. The generator includes the input dimensions at its top; regenerate in a working folder containing a StandKit subfolder using FreeCAD's bundled Python. CAD checks: valid single solids and zero positive-volume overlaps between modeled parts at the saved pose. Loads, physical fit and print tolerances have not been tested.
