# Nominal clearances

| Feature | Nominal gap (mm) | Manufacturing tolerance | Reason |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Display housing to side guides | 0.600 per side | Pending measured housing and process | Vertical insertion |
| Mast in square sockets | 0.300 per side | Pending tube stock and socket process | Assembly before cross-bolting |
| Paw body in dock | 0.600 per side | Pending taper and printed fit sample | Hand removal |
| Camera between front and rear fences | 0.600 per side in depth | Pending housing measurement | Seating before straps are tightened |
| 8 mm pivot in 8.5 mm rotor bore | 0.250 radial | Pending bolt and finished bore | Horizontal pan without binding |

Each value is calculated from the nominal CAD dimensions. The saved assembly pose does not establish clearance throughout camera motion.
